To acknowledge AGINPLACE in publications, please use any of the following sentences, using the reference of your subproject. <b>This sentence must be mentioned in the first place of the acknowledgement</b> as required by the Ministry.

<p>If the paper is in <b>English</b>:</p>

<ul>
<li>UVa: Grant PID2023-146254OB-C41 funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by ERDF/EU.</li>
<li>USAL: Grant PID2023-146254OB-C42 funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by ERDF/EU.</li>
<li>UAH: Grant PID2023-146254OB-C43 funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by ERDF/EU. </li>
<li>UD: Grant PID2023-146254OA-C44 funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by ERDF/EU.  </li>
</ul>

If multiple partners are authors of the publication, please use all of their references, as for example:
Grants PID2023-146254OB-C41 and PID2023-146254OA-C44 funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by ERDF/EU.

<p>If the paper is in <b>Spanish</b>:</p>

<ul>
<li>UVa:  Proyecto PID2023-146254OB-C41 financiado por MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 y por FEDER/UE.</li>
<li>USAL: Proyecto PID2023-146254OB-C42 financiado por MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 y por FEDER/UE.</li>
<li>UAH: Proyecto PID2023-146254OB-C43 financiado por MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 y por FEDER/UE.</li>
<li>UD: Proyecto PID2023-146254OA-C44 financiado por MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 y por FEDER/UE.</li>
</ul>

And for multiple partners use all the references, as for example: Proyectos PID2023-146254OB-C41 y PID2023-146254OA-C44 financiados por MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 y por FEDER/UE.

On Thursday, 16 October 2025, Alfonso Bahillo (Universidad de Valladolid) presented the paper “Home-installed sensors for enhanced caregiving: noise and bed presence sensors” at the 5th International Conference on Electrical, Computer, Communications and Mechatronics Engineering (ICECCME 2025), held from October 16–19 in Zanzibar, Tanzania. 

The work presented an important step forward in the development of adaptive, reliable home monitoring solutions aimed at improving user well-being and reducing care-related costs. 

The presentation showcased research results developed at the Universidad de Valladolid within the AGINPLACE project.


More details: https://www.iceccme.com/2025/

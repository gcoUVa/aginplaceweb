On Thursday, 15 October 2026, Alfonso Bahillo (Universidad de Valladolid) presented the paper “Annotated dataset of simulated voiding sounds impacting ceramic surface for urine flow estimation” at the 6th International Conference on Electrical, Computer, Communications and Mechatronics Engineering (ICECCME 2026), held from October 15–17 in Bali, Indonesia. 

The work presented a dataset of simulated male voiding sounds recorded as a standing stream strikes the ceramic bowl of a conventional floor-mounted toilet. This provides a clean baseline that can be augmented with background noise when training AI models for use in real-world settings. 

The presentation showcased research results developed at the Universidad de Valladolid within the AGINPLACE project.

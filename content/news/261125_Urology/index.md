We are seeking urology departments and centers interested in joining our multicenter study aimed at validating the generalizability of our artificial intelligence model. Your center can help us take the next step: establish sound-based uroflowmetry as a reliable alternative to conventional uroflowmetry, paving the way for future home-based monitoring. We are exploring a new way to capture key urodynamic parameters through artificial intelligence, directly from the comfort of the patient’s home. This research has already yielded impactful outcomes in the form of Scientific Publications and Research Projects. Now, we are ready to take the next step: expanding our research through a multicenter study. This will allow us to increase the sample size, collect data from different clinical centers, and assess the generalizability of our findings, helping lay the groundwork for a future phase focused on home monitoring.


For more information contact: alfonso.bahillo@uva.es

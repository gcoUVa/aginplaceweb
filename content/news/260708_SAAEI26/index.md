Researchers from the AGINPLACE-UAH subproject participated in the 31st Annual Seminar on Automation, Industrial Electronics and Instrumentation (SAAEI 2026), held in Gijón from 8-10 July, where members of the group presented four contributions on advanced technologies for indoor monitoring and assisted living. The works addressed wearable-based UWB positioning and machine learning for routine extraction, ultrasonic device-free localization, non-intrusive estimation of sleep fragmentation indicators at home, and hybrid pose estimation in infrared positioning systems.
Together, these contributions advance the development of accurate, unobtrusive and privacy-preserving solutions for monitoring people in domestic environments, supporting the main objectives of AGINPLACE-UAH. During the conference, members of the group also participated in the meeting of the Spanish Chapter of the IEEE Instrumentation and Measurement Society, providing an opportunity to share results, exchange knowledge and strengthen scientific collaboration in the field.

In July 2025, Mario Robles, a researcher hired within the AgeInPlace-UAH subproject (Universidad de Alcalá), presented the paper “Seguimiento de Adultos Mayores en sus hogares mediante Técnicas de Clustering (Monitoring Older Adults at Home Using Clustering Techniques)” at the 2025 Symposium on Industrial Electronics Applications (SAAEI 2025), held in Aranjuez, Spain, from 2-4 July. 
The work, developed by researchers from the University of Alcalá in collaboration with ViveLibre, explores the use of unsupervised machine learning techniques to analyze indoor localization data and model the daily routines of older adults living independently at home.
The proposed system combines Ultra-Wideband (UWB) positioning technology with clustering and classification algorithms to automatically identify the main areas occupied by a user within the home and track behavioral patterns over time. Experimental results demonstrate the potential of this approach for the early detection of routine changes that may be associated with physical or cognitive decline, contributing to the development of non-intrusive technologies that support healthy and independent ageing in place.

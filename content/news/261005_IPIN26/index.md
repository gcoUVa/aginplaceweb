Researchers from the AGINPLACE-UAH subproject presented two contributions at IEEE International Conference on Indoor Positioning and Indoor Navigation (IPIN 2026) held from 5-8 October, advancing the development of unobtrusive localization technologies for people monitoring.
Elena Aparicio presented “Design of a Multi-Channel Infrared Transmitter Architecture for Local Positioning Systems”, related to Task 5, which makes progress on high-accuracy indoor positioning systems based on infrared technology for tracking human movement. 
Gabriel García Gutierrez presented “Device-Free Indoor Localization with Low-Cost COTS mmWave Presence Sensors”, associated with Task 4, proposing a low-cost localization solution based on mmWave radar presence sensors. The system estimates people location within the home in a minimally intrusive way, demonstrating its potential for monitoring and assisted living applications.

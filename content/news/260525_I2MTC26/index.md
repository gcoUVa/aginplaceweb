Researchers from the AGINPLACE-UAH subproject presented two contributions at the IEEE International Instrumentation and Measurement Technology Conference (I2MTC 2026) held from 25-28 May 2026. Both works advance the project’s goal of developing technologies for unobtrusive and reliable monitoring at home.
Mario Robles-Salamanca presented “Clustering-based Tracking Model for Older Adults in Indoor Environments”, linked to Task 5 on wearable-based monitoring. The work combines UWB localization and machine learning to identify daily routines and detect potential behavioral changes. Alejandro García-Requejo presented “Device-Free Localization and Tracking using Sonar and Shadowing-based Ultrasonic Measurements”, linked to Task 4. This approach uses complementary ultrasonic systems to track people indoors accurately and unobtrusively, without requiring them to wear any device.


More details: https://i2mtc2026.ieee-ims.org/

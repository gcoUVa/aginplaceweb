In September 2026, Mario Robles, researcher of the AgeInPlace-UAH subproject, presented his Master's Thesis entitled “Analysis of Daily Routines through a Hybrid IoT System Based on Wearable and Passive Sensors, achieving the highest distinction. The master thesis was supervised by researchers from AGINPLACE-UAH subproject, Mª del Carmen Pérez Rubio and Álvaro Hernández Alonso.
The work explores the use of unsupervised machine learning techniques to analyze indoor localization data and model the daily routines of older adults living independently at home. The proposed system showed promising results for the early detection of routine changes that may be associated with functional or cognitive decline. The solution was validated using wearable UWB localization technology and mmWave radar sensing, highlighting the potential of indoor localization data to support ambient assisted living and healthy aging.
